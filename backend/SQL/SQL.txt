Why not RANK() for second highest?

Because RANK() skips numbers after a tie. **********important********************

For example:

80,000 → RANK 1
70,000 → RANK 2
70,000 → RANK 2
60,000 → RANK 4

There is no RANK 3.

But DENSE_RANK() does not skip:          **************important**************************

80,000 → 1
70,000 → 2
70,000 → 2
60,000 → 3
